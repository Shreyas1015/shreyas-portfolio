## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leads and recruiters evaluating Shreyas Gurav for software engineering roles, plus engineers who arrive from his open-source packages. They skim on a laptop between other candidates, or open the link on a phone from a message or LinkedIn.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Shreyas Gurav, Software Engineer in Mumbai. It shows what he has built and run in production (Aerem, Schbang), his two open-source npm packages, his stack and his AWS certification, and makes contact one click away. Success: a visitor leaves knowing what he builds, believing the numbers, and with his email or resume in hand.

## Positioning

Production full-stack engineer for fintech and marketplace products (Node.js, Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, AWS, LLM/RAG) who also authors tools that keep AI coding agents honest: codeloom and create-rigel.
