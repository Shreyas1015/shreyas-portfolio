# Experience section

Scope: the Experience section of the single-page portfolio (src/components/experience.tsx + the Impact reel inside it). Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: hiring managers and engineers skimming on a laptop or phone. They must leave with the two companies and the numbers in equal weight.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The career as one two-page spread, read left to right from Schbang to Aerem, then proven by a pinned sideways reel of numbers. It refuses the category default of a dated job list with bullet points, and refuses cards, carousels and accordions.

OWN-WORLD: The site concept's world, unchanged: warm black #0f0e0d and cream paper #f1ede6 as the two panel grounds, signal orange #ff4f2e only for figures and the reel's progress, Funnel Display set huge and tight, Funnel Sans body, hairline rules. Numbers live only in the reel.

STORY: The visitor sees where Shreyas has been and is now, reads one plain paragraph per company about what he owned, then scrolls through ten measured results and believes them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width spread. Left half cream: dates top, "Schbang" ~150px bottom-anchored, role, two-line paragraph. Right half dark: same for "Aerem". The reel's first figures start below the spread.

FORM: Then and now, side by side (position 6 of 7 on the ordered list), seed:bafad3f279361afb.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
